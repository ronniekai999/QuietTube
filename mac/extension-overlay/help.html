QuietTube for Mac

YouTube, with fewer interruptions.

Connect Safari

  1. Open QuietTube.app from Applications.
  2. Choose Safari settings in QuietTube, then enable the QuietTube extension.
  3. Allow QuietTube on youtube.com. Open the toolbar popup and check for Mac app connected.
  4. Reload YouTube and play a video.

Safari 18.6 or later is required by this filtering engine. This preview is signed for local development; a public download needs Developer ID signing and notarization.

Connect Chrome

  1. Choose Connect Chrome in QuietTube to register its local companion.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and enable Developer mode.
  3. Choose Load unpacked and select the Chrome extension folder shown by QuietTube.
  4. Pin QuietTube, open its popup, check for Mac app connected, and reload YouTube.

The app should stay in Applications so Chrome can find its companion after a restart.

Your controls

Turn off stops QuietTube filtering. Pause 15 minutes resumes automatically. The Mac app and connected browser profiles share these settings. Reload an already open video after a change.

Closing the setup window keeps the menu bar app running. Quitting the app leaves each extension using its saved protection setting.

If something needs attention

Check that the extension is enabled and allowed on YouTube. Reload the video after changing protection. Use Export diagnostics in QuietTube if a problem persists; exports contain settings and filtering acknowledgments, without browsing history, cookies, or video URLs.

Filters and scripts are packaged locally. This build does not download executable JavaScript. YouTube can change its player, so active filtering does not guarantee every ad is blocked.

Updates and removal

Quit QuietTube, replace its app in Applications, reopen it, and reload the Chrome extension. Saved protection settings stay in the shared QuietTube app group.

Remove QuietTube from Chrome, disable it in Safari, and use Disconnect Chrome and disable Launch at login before moving the app to Trash. QuietTube only removes a Chrome registration that belongs to this app.
